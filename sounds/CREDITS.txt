Sounds used by Helm Chess
============================================================

Every file here is Creative Commons 0 (public domain), taken
from Freesound. No attribution is legally required, but the
people who made them deserve the credit anyway.

Each file has been folded to dual mono, trimmed of silence,
and normalised so they all sit at the same level.

Picking a piece up has no sound yet, on purpose.
